Places on Earth that don't feel real 🤯 #travel #nature #earth

Places on Earth that don't feel real, but every one of them is real. Which one would you visit? Comment the number 👇

1. Mount Bromo, Indonesia: An active volcano in a sea of sand. Sunrise here happens above the clouds.
2. Victoria Falls, Zambia & Zimbabwe: Locally called 'The Smoke That Thunders'. Its spray can be seen from far away.
3. Machu Picchu, Peru: A 15th-century Inca citadel. Built high in the Andes mountains.
4. Wadi Rum, Jordan: Known as the Valley of the Moon. Films have used it to stand in for Mars.
5. Lapland: Northern lights over snowy forests. Far above the Arctic Circle.
6. Bryce Canyon, USA: Thousands of rock spires called hoodoos. Shaped by frost and erosion.
